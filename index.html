Where did your property tax go in FY2025?

New Bedford, Massachusetts  ·  Fiscal Year 2025 (audited actual results)

Your property tax doesn’t pay for all of city government — far from it. This receipt shows what it does pay for: how your tax is spread across city services, after state aid, federal grants, and fees are subtracted from what each one costs.

Homeowners aren’t the only property taxpayers. Commercial, industrial, and personal property carry about 28% of the City’s tax levy, taxed at $22.85 per $1,000 — just over twice the $11.31 residential rate. Rental housing is taxed at the residential rate, so renters carry a large share of the tax bill through their rent.

Built into every line above

Pensions and health insurance aren’t billed separately — they sit inside each function’s cost. In FY2025 the City put $40.7M toward employee pensions and about $36.6M toward health and related insurance: close to $77M together, near a tenth of all City spending. The retiree-health share is almost entirely unfunded — the City reports roughly $510M in long-term retiree-health (OPEB) obligations, with under 2% set aside.
